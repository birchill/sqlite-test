Reading the estimates

Storage estimates cover this site as a whole, including both backends. The usage change is the difference between the displayed before and after measurements. Load one backend at a time to compare changes.

Loading first clears that backend’s existing data. Delete data clears its tables and compacts the database; browsers may take time to update their estimates.
